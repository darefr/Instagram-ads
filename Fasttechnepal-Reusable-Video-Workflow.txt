H. MOTION DESIGN AND TIMELINE ENGINEERING

- This must feel like motion graphics, not five static slides with transitions.
- Use purposeful kinetic typography, responsive-screen movement, staggered
  labels, click interactions and limited depth.
- Default text entrances: approximately 0.4–0.7 seconds, 30–80 px travel,
  cubic ease-out or equivalent, modest opacity change.
- Stagger related text layers by roughly 0.1–0.2 seconds, not several seconds.
- Default scene transition: approximately 0.25–0.35 seconds. Keep headline
  text readable; avoid flashes, violent zooms and excessive motion blur.
- Give long passages adequate reading time. Do not spend most of a scene
  animating text before it can be read.
- Start the hook immediately. Do not use a separate long logo intro.
- Keep the last contact card stable after its initial reveal.
- Use real masks/layers and independent motion, not repeated whole-frame
  pans as the only animation.
- For code rendering, make the timeline deterministic and render by frame/time,
  not by wall-clock browser timing that can drop frames.
- At 30 seconds and 30 fps, verify a total of 900 frames.
- Make at least five representative still proofs BEFORE the expensive render.
- Inspect the first frame, fully settled scenes, transitions and end card.
